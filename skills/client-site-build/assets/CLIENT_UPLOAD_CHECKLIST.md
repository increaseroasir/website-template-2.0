# Photo Upload Checklist — <CLIENT NAME>

Upload your photos to the shared Google Drive folder. **Start each filename
with the LABEL below** (capitalization doesn't matter, and anything after
the label is fine — `hero-sunset-2.jpg` works). We'll take it from there.

Phone photos are great — just send the **original full-size file** (no
screenshots, no texting them to yourself first; that shrinks them).

## Must-haves (the site needs these 6)

| # | Filename starts with | What to shoot (plain language) | Orientation | Minimum size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `HERO` | Your best "wow" shot — a beautiful tub/spa in a real backyard or your showroom centerpiece. This is the first thing every visitor sees. | Landscape (wider than tall) | 1600 × 1000 |
| 2 | `HOTTUBS` | Your best hot tub — clean, filled, cover off | Portrait (taller than wide) | 1000 × 1250 |
| 3 | `SWIMSPAS` | Your best swim spa | Portrait | 1000 × 1250 |
| 4 | `SAUNAS` | Your best sauna (door open, lit, inviting) | Portrait | 1000 × 1250 |
| 5 | `SHOWROOM1` | Inside your showroom — the view a customer gets walking in | Landscape | 1200 × 900 |
| 6 | `SHOWROOM2` | A second showroom angle, or your team helping a customer | Landscape | 1200 × 900 |

## Nice to have (the site works without these, but they help)

| Filename starts with | What to shoot | Orientation | Minimum size |
|---|---|---|---|
| `LOGO` | Your logo file — the original from your designer if you have it (SVG/PNG with transparent background is ideal) | — | 320 × 80 |
| `LOGOLIGHT` | A white/light version of your logo (it sits on a dark navy footer) | — | 320 × 80 |
| `HEROHOTTUBS` | A wide hot-tub lifestyle shot (tops the Hot Tubs page) | Landscape | 1600 × 1000 |
| `HEROSWIMSPAS` | A wide swim-spa shot (tops the Swim Spas page) | Landscape | 1600 × 1000 |
| `HEROSAUNAS` | A wide sauna shot (tops the Saunas page) | Landscape | 1600 × 1000 |

**Tips:** shoot in daylight or bright showroom light · wipe water spots ·
step back so the whole unit is in frame · more photos is better — upload
extras and we'll pick the best.
